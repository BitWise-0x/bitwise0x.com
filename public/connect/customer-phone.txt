To view a BitWise desktop from your phone:

1. Install "RustDesk Remote Desktop" (App Store or Google Play).
2. Copy this whole line:
   0nI9s2dyV3Q3kld0hzQTp0VvNFSLR0K4lVOrYVWrsya2klMtB3S1MlWORjNVRkI6ISeltmIsISbvNmL4BTZzl2d0lmYus2clRGdzVncv8iOzBHd0hmI6ISawFmIsISbvNmL4BTZzl2d0lmYus2clRGdzVnciojI0N3boJye
3. In RustDesk open the Settings tab, tap "ID/Relay Server", then "Import Server Config". It fills itself from what you copied. Tap OK.
4. Still in Settings, switch on "Use WebSocket".
5. Open the Connection tab, enter the ID we give you, and tap Connect.

Your own status in the app stays on "Connecting"; that is normal and does not stop you from connecting to us.
